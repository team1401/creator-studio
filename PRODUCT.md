# Creator Studio

## Register
product

## Users
Brian, working privately across a phone and desktop, with agents assisting bounded production work. A project is scoped to this studio, never the global task boards.

## Product Purpose
Keep character references, video and product drafts, assets, service links, human decisions and production handoffs together. The primary question is: what needs me, why, and what do I do next? Start with an empty real workspace. Brian chooses the first character and niche inside onboarding.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, practical. A working studio rather than a growth dashboard. No invented success, optimistic integration status or decorative analytics.

## Anti-references
No generic metric-card wall, neon agent console, fabricated examples presented as work, or autonomous-publishing claims. Distinguish an external website link from a connected integration.

## Design Principles
1. Human decisions have explicit instructions and next steps.
2. Drafts and manual records never imply actual generation or publication.
3. Persist actual work, approvals and provenance.
4. Keep paid generation, storefront deployment and publishing outside this first slice.
5. Keep the first usable workflow short: character, project, asset, review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use native labeled form controls, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support and mobile layouts. These are implementation targets, not a claim of a formal WCAG audit.

## Provenance and scope
Derived from the authorized working-slice brief and creator-studio-discovery/video-analysis.md, especially the dashboard inference and human decision boundaries. Visual choices are reversible implementation decisions pending Brian's review, not a claim of separately approved branding.
